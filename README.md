# Brazilian · Agile Leader · Technical Project Manager · Professional Cat Herder

Based in Denver, CO, I lead complex technology programs that deliver reliable, human-centered systems and high-performing teams.  

Raised in São Paulo, I’ve lived in Uruguay, the Netherlands, New York, and San Francisco before settling in Denver nearly a decade ago. My international experience shapes the way I approach problem-solving, collaboration, and team leadership.  

I speak Portuguese, English, and Spanish — currently learning Russian. My love of languages shapes my volunteer work and helps me bridge technical and business teams across cultures.  

I’ve volunteered at film festivals since 2005 and at a Denver farm animal sanctuary since 2016, providing guest relations, translation, and animal care — showing up for who can’t advocate for themselves.  

At home, I share my space with four cats: Gremlin, Genghis Khan, Jonesy, and Ghost. Together, they’ve taught me diplomacy, negotiation, and resource management like no team ever could.  

Explore my projects below or reach out — I love connecting over technology, process improvement, and yes, cat pictures.
